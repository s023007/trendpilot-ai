# Audience targeting

## Primary organic audiences
- United Kingdom: ticket + stadium + nearby hotel intent.
- United Arab Emirates: Dubai/Abu Dhabi to Manchester football-weekend intent.
- Saudi Arabia: Riyadh/Jeddah to Manchester football-weekend intent.

## Secondary
- Qatar, Kuwait, Oman: event-led travel planning when route-specific content is genuinely useful.
- Europe: short-haul ticket + hotel intent.

## Distribution rule
Search pages first. Site/YouTube video second. Pinterest derivatives third. High-frequency social posting is optional, not required.
