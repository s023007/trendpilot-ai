Before checkout

Four details that change the true booking value.

A cheap headline price is not enough when fees, seat location or delivery details are unclear.

1

Exact event

Confirm the date, city, venue and local start time. Event schedules can change.

2

Seat evidence

Read the block, row, adjacency and restricted-view wording for the exact listing.

3

Total cost

Check service fees, taxes, delivery and currency conversion before calling one offer cheaper.

4

Delivery & protection

Confirm ticket format, delivery timing, event-change rules and buyer-protection terms.
