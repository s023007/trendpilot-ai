General football seating guide

Understand the trade-off before choosing a block.

Exact stadium layouts differ. Always use the block, row, seat, restricted-view and supporter-area information shown for the particular match.

Lower

Lower tier / close seats

More detail and proximity, but the full shape of play can be harder to read from very low rows.

  • Best for close action
  • Often higher priced
  • Check row height and sightline
Middle

Long side / balanced view

A common starting point for seeing both ends and following team shape without being too distant.

  • Balanced field view
  • Often strong value
  • Compare centre vs corner location
Upper

Upper tier

A wider tactical view and often a lower headline price, with more distance from players and atmosphere.

  • Wide perspective
  • Budget starting point
  • Check stairs, access and distance
Goal

Behind goal

Strong atmosphere and a direct view of one attacking end, but depth can be harder to judge at the far end.

  • Supporter energy
  • One-end focus
  • Confirm home/away restrictions
VIP

Hospitality

Comfort and services may be included, but packages vary widely and can contain conditions beyond the seat itself.

  • Comfort and amenities
  • Highest price range
  • Verify exactly what is included
Before you pay

Seven checks that prevent booking surprises.

These are decision prompts—not claims that a particular listing has a problem.

1

Exact match and date

Confirm teams, competition, venue, city and local event time. Match dates can move.

2

Seat location

Read block, row, category and any restricted or partial-view wording.

3

Seats together

For multiple tickets, verify whether adjacent seating is guaranteed or only requested.

4

Total checkout cost

Compare ticket price, service fees, taxes, delivery and currency conversion—not the headline alone.

5

Ticket delivery

Check electronic or physical delivery, expected timing and any mobile-app requirement.

6

Supporter restrictions

Some competitions restrict away-team colours, nationality, age or supporter sections.

7

Refund and event changes

Read provider rules for postponement, cancellation, venue changes and buyer-requested cancellation.
