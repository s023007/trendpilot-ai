Provider strategy
- Awin: retail brands, fashion, beauty, home and specialist stores when coverage is weak.
- CJ: established retailers and software brands when advertiser access is approved.
- eBay: rare, used, refurbished, discontinued products and replacement parts.
- Do not add a provider merely to increase raw count; prioritise unique products, usable images, accurate prices, shipping and stable affiliate links.
