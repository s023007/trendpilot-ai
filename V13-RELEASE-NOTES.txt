TrendPilot AI V13 — Shopping Decision Engine
=============================================

Purpose
-------
V13 changes TrendPilot from a repeated category-search website into a decision engine.
The buyer journey is now: find the exact product family, inspect the evidence, compare
compatible products, check savings, and save products for later.

Main changes
------------
1. Exact catalogue segments
   - Products are indexed by category, exact family and audience.
   - Example: apparel | t-shirts | men.
   - Result pages are split into 240-product shards and load progressively.
   - The previous 90-result ceiling has been removed.

2. Strict intent handling
   - T-shirt synonyms include T-shirt, tshirt, tee, crew-neck tee, short-sleeve tee,
     cotton tee and graphic tee.
   - Audience is determined from explicit fields, title and category—not marketing copy.
   - Description text cannot force an exact product family.
   - Exercise bikes, phone cases, pet products and children's garments cannot fill a
     men's T-shirt result set.

3. Exact matches and alternatives are separate
   - Exact family/audience results are the default.
   - Related alternatives use a separate tab and are never mixed silently.

4. Variant clustering and merchant evidence
   - Obvious size/colour variants are grouped under one product record.
   - Product cards can show number of offers/stores, rating, delivery, shipping and total
     supplied cost where the feeds provide those fields.

5. New homepage
   - One main search box.
   - Five decision actions: Find, Compare, Verify a saving, Watch a price, Rare Finds.
   - Distinct tools instead of repeated category search pages.
   - Compact live-product, savings and rare-find previews.

6. Distinct page functions
   - Find: exact product discovery and progressive catalogue loading.
   - Compare: a saved same-family comparison table.
   - Price Watch: local product watch list and target price storage.
   - Savings Center: product-linked seller markdown evidence first; coupon center second.
   - Electronics Lab: compatibility/use intake.
   - Software Finder: workflow, platform and licence intake.
   - Business Sourcing: MOQ, sample and customisation intake.
   - Rare Finds Radar: scarce used/refurbished/open-box products only.
   - Buying Guides: short decision checks, not repeated category introductions.

7. Mobile redesign
   - Global horizontal overflow safeguards.
   - Compact two-column product cards on small phones.
   - One-line product action button.
   - Responsive filters, compare table and watch list.

8. Feed coverage reporting
   - Source offers, accepted offers and clustered unique products.
   - Counts by group, family, audience and advertiser.
   - Missing image, price, rating, reviews, delivery, shipping, material and audience.
   - Exact example counts for men's/women's/kids' T-shirts and men's shorts.
   - Rejection reasons by advertiser.

9. Update workflow integration
   - The installer patches update-products.yml so the V13 decision catalogue is rebuilt
     after feed ingestion and before product changes are committed.

Honest limitations in this release
----------------------------------
- Seller old-price fields are labelled as seller markdown evidence, not verified 90-day
  price history. Verified historical grading requires collecting snapshots over time.
- Price Watch and targets are stored on the current device. Email/push alerts require a
  future account and notification service.
- Shipping, material, rating and delivery remain "not supplied" when feeds do not provide
  them. V13 does not invent missing evidence.


V13.1 INSTALL HOTFIX
- The installer no longer edits .github/workflows/update-products.yml from inside GitHub Actions.
- Future feed refreshes are connected through automation/build_search_catalog.py instead.
- This avoids GitHub rejecting the Commit V13 push while preserving automatic V13 catalogue rebuilds.
- The feed-refresh queue is advisory: an inability to queue it will not roll back a successful installation.
