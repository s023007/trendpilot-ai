Trust & transparency

Editorial methodology

How TrendPilot selects topics, studies competitors, scores evidence and separates editorial ranking from commission.

Page gate

A page must show search demand, buying intent, a defensible content gap, reliable sources and an update plan before publication.

Competitor research

We study the current search results in the target country, high-authority pages, retailer pages, official policies, specialist communities and public traffic estimates. Traffic estimates are directional and never presented as audited analytics.

Evidence labels

  • Hands-on tested: TrendPilot performed and documented the test.
  • Officially confirmed: the product or platform published the fact.
  • Independently reported: credible third parties tested or documented it.
  • User-reported: recurring user evidence, clearly labelled as such.
  • Editorial inference: a conclusion derived from cited evidence.

Ranking

Commission is not part of the public score. We consider fit, compatibility, evidence quality, total cost, seller or vendor reliability, support, return economics and update risk.

Feeds and automation

Feeds are sources of product data, not a license to create thousands of thin pages. A public product button must resolve to an exact product or official product page.
