How to choose in ten minutes

  1. Write the type of video you make most often.
  2. Choose the device you will actually edit on.
  3. Confirm the free export resolution and watermark rules.
  4. Check captions, screen recording and stock assets only if you use them weekly.
  5. Complete the same short project in your top two choices.

Why Filmora is not automatically first for everyone

TrendPilot has an active Wondershare affiliate relationship, but commission is excluded from editorial scoring. A mobile social creator will often be better served by CapCut, an Apple user with simple needs by iMovie, and a technically ambitious learner by DaVinci Resolve.

The hidden beginner costs

Look beyond the headline plan. Check stock-asset renewals, AI credits, cloud storage, premium templates, watermark restrictions and whether a perpetual license includes future major versions.
