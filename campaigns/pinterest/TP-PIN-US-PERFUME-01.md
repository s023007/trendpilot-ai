# TP-PIN-US-PERFUME-01

## Goal
Paid Pinterest test for BellaVita Her N’More using TrendPilot Choice as the decision landing page.

## Landing page
https://trendpilotchoice.com/offers/bellavita-her-n-more/

## Platform setup
- Platform: Pinterest Ads
- Campaign creation: Manual
- Objective: Consideration
- Optimization: Outbound clicks
- Bidding: Pinterest Performance+ bidding (required when optimizing for outbound clicks)
- Budget type: Lifetime
- Initial test cap: USD 15 total
- Test window: 3 days
- Geo: United States only
- Age: 18–44
- Gender: All genders
- Devices: All

## Targeting themes
Use keyword + interest targeting around:
- perfume gift set

- perfume sampler for women
- signature scent
- scent layering
- fragrance layering

## Measurement
Primary:
- Outbound CTR from Pinterest
- Cost per outbound click to TrendPilot

Secondary:
- TrendPilot affiliate outbound clicks
- Landing-page scroll depth
- Video engagement where available
- Creative-level CTR

## Initial decision rules
Do not judge from impressions alone.
- If a creative receives meaningful delivery but produces no outbound clicks, pause it first.
- If one creative clearly has lower cost per outbound click and stronger TrendPilot CTA activity, shift the remaining budget toward it.
- Do not scale the campaign until TrendPilot affiliate-outbound activity is visible from paid Pinterest traffic.
- Do not treat saves or closeups as the primary success metric; paid traffic must progress toward the seller.

## Creative specs
- Standard image Pin
- Recommended Pinterest ratio: 2:3
- Target canvas: 1000 × 1500 px
- PNG preferred
- Keep key copy away from bottom UI area
- Short overlay, strong product visibility, mobile-first readability

## Compliance notes
- Do not mention CJ or affiliate network names in visible ads.
- Do not use fake scarcity or unsupported discount claims.
- Landing page contains a concise sponsored-link disclosure.
- Final price, stock, delivery and return terms are confirmed on the seller page.
