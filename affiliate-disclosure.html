Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound links can earn TrendPilot a commission.

That commercial relationship helps support the site, but it does not change the requirement that a product result must be relevant and a seller destination must be described truthfully.

How affiliate links work

Some links to retailers, marketplaces, ticket providers, software companies or other services contain affiliate tracking. If you complete an eligible purchase or action after following such a link, TrendPilot may receive a commission.

Your checkout is with the provider

TrendPilot does not process the transaction. Prices, stock, shipping, taxes, warranty, returns, ticket availability and final terms are controlled by the provider and can change.

Direct versus broad destinations

Where the source provides evidence for the exact product destination, the site can label it accordingly. When only a broader marketplace, category or search destination is available, TrendPilot should not present that route as an exact product link.

Editorial separation

Affiliate eligibility is not proof of product quality or identity. Search relevance, product-role filtering and seller-policy rules are applied independently.

Following an affiliate link does not create a purchase obligation. Always verify the product and final price on the provider's site before payment.
