Affiliate disclosure

Some links may earn revenue from a qualifying click or purchase. The visitor pays no extra fee because of this disclosure.

Revenue models

CPC can pay for a qualifying outbound click. CPA can pay after a qualifying purchase or action. A programme can support one or both models.

Editorial independence

Commission, CPC rate, approval probability and advertiser preference are excluded from editorial ranking. A page can recommend an option that does not pay TrendPilot.

Link treatment

Sponsored links use clear destination language and the attributes rel="sponsored nofollow noopener". We do not use forced redirects, hidden links, misleading download buttons, click incentives or automatic clicks.

Exact product rule

Feed-based commerce links must resolve to the relevant product detail page before publication. When an approved software deeplink is not yet available, the page can use the official product page as a clearly identified fallback.

Click measurement

TrendPilot can record the page, placement, advertiser, product, device context and revenue model of an outbound affiliate click. This helps identify useful pages and rejected or low-quality traffic. No click is generated without the visitor choosing the link.

Prices and terms

Prices, shipping, availability, tax, renewals and return terms can change. The destination page and checkout are the controlling sources.
