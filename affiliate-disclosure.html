How links work

Some shopping links can support TrendPilot.

You do not pay extra. The recommendation is written around product fit, limitations and buyer risk—not the amount a seller may pay.

What happens when you click

A retailer or software company may recognise that the visit came from TrendPilot. Depending on its rules, TrendPilot may receive a small payment after the click or after an eligible purchase.

What does not change

The payment does not increase your price, does not create an automatic click and does not decide which product appears first.

Where a button should take you

Product buttons should open the relevant product, plan or pricing page. We avoid hidden redirects, forced clicks and misleading download buttons.

What you should recheck

Price, shipping, tax, renewals, return terms, compatibility and stock can change. The retailer page and checkout are the final sources before payment.

Click measurement

TrendPilot may record the page, button position, seller and product name to understand which guides are useful and to investigate broken destinations. A click is recorded only after you choose the link.

Ticket links

How ticket-provider links work.

Clear outbound action

Event images and names open TrendPilot decision pages. A clearly labelled provider button is the action that leaves TrendPilot.

Marketplace disclosure

Official sellers and resale marketplaces are identified separately. A marketplace is not described as official without listing-level evidence.

Affiliate relationship

TrendPilot may earn a commission or qualifying referral payment from provider links. Organic comparison order is based on available booking evidence, not the highest payment.
