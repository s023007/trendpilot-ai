Affiliate disclosure

How CPC and CPA links support TrendPilot without controlling editorial recommendations.

How TrendPilot may earn

Some links are affiliate links. A programme may pay for a qualified click (CPC), a completed purchase or action (CPA), or another disclosed commission model. The visitor does not pay TrendPilot directly.

Editorial independence

Commission rate is not part of the editorial score. We may recommend a non-affiliate option when it fits the user better.

Link behavior

Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener". We do not open store links automatically, disguise them as downloads or ask users to click merely to support the site.

Prices and policies

Prices, stock, shipping, return terms, software plans and discounts can change. The seller or official checkout page is the final source.
