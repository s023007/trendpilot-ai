TrendPilot AI V13.2 — Calm Search & Mobile Reliability
=====================================================

Purpose
-------
This update fixes the visible mobile defects reported after V13 and redesigns the interface around a calmer marketplace-style experience inspired by the useful interaction patterns seen in Envato and Alpha Academy, without copying their branding.

Search and catalogue fixes
--------------------------
1. Result totals now count actual exact matches found, not every product inside a candidate catalogue segment.
2. Generic searches automatically continue checking relevant catalogue pages until 24 exact matches are found or all candidates are exhausted.
3. “School” can now show “1 exact match” when only one item truly matches instead of displaying a misleading 35.
4. Exact T-shirt searches load the full relevant product-family pages progressively and keep unrelated products out.
5. The Show More button reveals loaded matches first, then checks additional catalogue pages only when needed.
6. Search categories are separate from the typed query: All, Clothing, Electronics, Home, School & office, Sports, Beauty, Kids, Software and Business.
7. A curated search-suggestion panel appears when the search field is focused.

Mobile menu and navigation
--------------------------
1. The menu is now a solid, full-height, viewport-level panel with its own scrolling area.
2. The hero/page underneath is darkened by a separate backdrop and can no longer visually mix with menu links.
3. Menu links are grouped into Decide and Explore sections.
4. A mobile bottom navigation bar adds Home, Search, Deals and Compare.
5. Compare counters are shared between the header and bottom bar.

Deals and coupons
-----------------
1. Product-linked savings remain the first section.
2. Coupons default to worldwide/unspecified English records.
3. Oman and All countries are available as filters.
4. All languages can be enabled deliberately instead of flooding the page by default.
5. Long titles and descriptions wrap and are clamped so cards cannot extend beyond the screen.
6. Country/language badges and a Show all coupons control were added.

Visual redesign
---------------
1. Replaced the heavy dark/white repetition with a calm cream, soft green, pale blue and white system.
2. Reduced oversized headings and excessive section heights.
3. Simplified the homepage to four primary decisions, current products, specialist tools, savings and transparency.
4. Rebuilt the search bar with an integrated category selector and suggestion menu.
5. Added a Netlify-compatible email signup section above the footer.
6. Made product cards more compact and balanced on narrow phones.
