TrendPilot V11 — Global Commerce Experience
============================================

Buyer-facing changes
--------------------
1. Rebuilt the homepage around a direct product search, not a collection of links.
2. Rebuilt the product finder with category, audience, seller, price, coupon and sort filters.
3. Added broad-category routing: clothing opens apparel, footwear, bags and fashion accessories;
   electronics opens phones, computers, audio, cameras, TV/projectors and smart-home products.
4. Expanded the feed taxonomy for dresses, tops, outerwear, knitwear, jeans, skirts, activewear,
   swimwear, underwear, sleepwear, shoes, bags, watches, eyewear, beauty, home and electronics.
5. Preserved women, men and kids as useful search and filter signals.
6. Increased the public search catalogue ceiling from 28,000 to 44,000 products and increased
   apparel/footwear/accessory capacity.
7. Added a short, dynamic buying guide to every result set instead of relying on a long article.
8. Added same-type comparison logic with a mobile-safe shortlist and full-screen mobile comparison.
9. Improved image handling: tiny, broken, placeholder and extreme-ratio images are replaced by a
   branded fallback instead of leaving blank space.
10. Matched current coupons beside relevant products and kept the searchable deals page.
11. Rebuilt the Electronics, Software, Business Sourcing and Comparisons hubs as useful decision hubs.
12. Added a unified global header/footer and upgraded older editorial pages with cleaner typography,
    a table of contents, reading progress and buyer-facing wording.
13. Rebuilt the sitemap and kept the existing feed and coupon automations connected.

Installation
------------
Upload this ZIP to the repository root. Upload install-trendpilot-v11-global.yml to
.github/workflows/, run it once, and wait for the automatically queued Update Products workflow.
