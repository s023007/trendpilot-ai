# TP-META-SA-MANCHESTER-DERBY-01

Saudi Arabia test campaign for Manchester United vs Manchester City on 13 September 2026.

## Launch gates

Do not spend on Meta until all of the following are true:

1. Exact-event affiliate deeplinks verified for TicketNetwork, Sports Events 365 and Ticombo.
2. Current availability checked on the same day the ad goes live.
3. Hotel cross-sell deeplink verified for Manchester on Trip.com.
4. `AffiliateOutboundClick` is observed in production analytics with seller/network/placement dimensions.
5. Mobile rendering reviewed on the production domain.
6. Meta billing safety limit confirmed.

## First test

- Market: Saudi Arabia
- Objective: Traffic / Landing Page Views
- Initial spend cap: USD 6
- Creatives: 2
- Primary decision metric: ticket outbound-click rate from the TrendPilot page
- Stop: weak or near-zero outbound intent after meaningful landing-page traffic
- Scale: only after qualified outbound intent is demonstrated
