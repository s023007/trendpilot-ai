Corrections policy

Material errors should be corrected clearly and as quickly as practical.

What to report

Report broken or misdirected links, outdated plans, incorrect compatibility statements, changed policies, unsupported claims or missing conflicts.

What to include

Send the page URL, the text in question, the reason it appears wrong and a current supporting source when available.

How corrections are handled

We review the evidence, update the page and its modified date when material, or remove the page from public navigation when the evidence cannot be restored.

Use the contact page and include “Correction” in the subject.
