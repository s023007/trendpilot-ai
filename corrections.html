Corrections policy

How to report an error and how TrendPilot records significant corrections.

Report an error

Use the contact page and include the page URL, the statement that appears wrong and a source that supports the correction.

What we correct

We correct factual errors, broken destinations, expired plan details, mismatched product variants and misleading wording. A material correction receives a note in the page update record.

What may change without being an error

Prices, stock, seller availability and promotions change frequently. Pages show a last-checked date and direct visitors to the current checkout.
