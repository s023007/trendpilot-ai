# Page research and publishing standard

A page is a standalone research project. Product-feed availability alone is not a publishing reason.

## Required pre-publication record

1. Primary query and search intent.
2. Target country and language.
3. Current search-results review, including the direct ranking pages rather than only large domains.
4. Competitor strengths, repeated structures and unresolved questions.
5. Original value TrendPilot can add.
6. Official sources and independent evidence.
7. Hands-on status: tested, research-based or user-reported.
8. Product/service eligibility and exact destination links.
9. CPC or CPA route strategy, documented without assuming link mode.
10. Internal-link plan and update cadence.

## Page readiness score

- Search demand: 15
- Commercial intent: 15
- Competitor gap: 20
- Original evidence/value: 20
- Decision usefulness: 10
- Monetisation fit: 10
- Maintainability: 10

Target: at least 80/100 before publication.

## Editorial rules

- Do not copy competitor wording or structure mechanically.
- Do not claim hands-on testing without evidence.
- State who should not buy the option.
- Record price and availability check dates.
- Use commission only for internal commercial prioritisation, never as the visible ranking score.
- Keep sources in the public page and detailed notes in its research record.
