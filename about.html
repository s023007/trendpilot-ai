About TrendPilot AI

How TrendPilot researches, compares and monetises product decisions.

What the site is

TrendPilot AI is a research-led comparison and product-discovery site. Its public pages combine editorial analysis with exact product links from confirmed affiliate programmes.

What the site is not

It is not a retailer, manufacturer or seller. We do not handle payment, delivery, returns or warranties. Those obligations belong to the store and seller shown at checkout.

Our publishing focus

We begin with active relationships for AliExpress, Joom, Geekbuying, Alibaba and Wondershare. Pending programmes are not shown publicly until approval and link verification are complete.

Use of automation and AI

Automation collects and normalises catalogue data, checks destination precision and flags opportunities. Editorial pages require human review, a research record and explicit statements about whether a product was hands-on tested.
