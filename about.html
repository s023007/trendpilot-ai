About TrendPilot AI

TrendPilot AI is a buyer-decision website for people comparing software, electronics, marketplaces and business sourcing options.

What we publish

We create focused comparisons and buying guides around fit, compatibility, total cost, support and risk. The goal is to help a visitor make a decision even when no outbound link is clicked.

What we do not claim

A guide based on documents and published evidence is not presented as a hands-on test. We do not invent ratings, performance results, prices or user reviews. Product feeds can support live offers, but they do not determine editorial winners.

How the site earns

Some links can generate revenue from eligible clicks or qualifying purchases. The commercial model is recorded for measurement and compliance, but it is excluded from editorial order.

Current scope

The public scope currently covers video-editing software, consumer marketplaces, specialist electronics and Alibaba sourcing. eSIM guides will be added when there are enough reliable plans and working destinations for a useful comparison.
