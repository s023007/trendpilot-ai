About TrendPilot

A product discovery and comparison site built around clearer buying decisions.

TrendPilot helps visitors search products, compare seller options, explore hard-to-find items and reach supported ticket or sourcing providers without pretending that incomplete seller data is complete.

What TrendPilot does

We organize product and seller data into shopper-friendly search, detail, comparison, deal, Rare Find, ticket and business-sourcing experiences. When a seller supplies a price, image, identifier or direct destination, TrendPilot can surface that information with appropriate context.

What TrendPilot is not

TrendPilot is not the seller, ticket issuer, payment processor or shipping provider. Stock, delivery, final checkout price, warranty, returns, ticket availability and seller terms must be confirmed with the provider before payment.

How the site is funded

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If a visitor completes an eligible purchase or action, TrendPilot may receive a commission. The goal is to keep that commercial relationship separate from the product identity and seller-truth rules used on the site.

Data can change

Marketplace feeds and affiliate sources can change after they are imported. TrendPilot therefore labels uncertain or broader destinations and avoids inventing missing prices, availability or ticket details.
