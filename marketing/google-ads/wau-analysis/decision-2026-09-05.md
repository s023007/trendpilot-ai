## Markets rejected for first test
- Romania: many relevant commercial terms showed CI 100 despite low bid estimates.
- Poland: large demand but most core product terms showed CI 98–100.
- UK/Ireland: core WAU categories were generally much more expensive and/or CI 100.
- Canada: strong demand for scalp-massager intent, but competition is materially higher than Bulgaria; keep as second-stage test.

## Current decision
**Best first-market hypothesis: Bulgaria.**

Best first product family: **scalp massager**, provided the selected WAU landing/product price and shipping eligibility support the economics.

Suggested first non-brand keyword set for further pre-launch validation:
