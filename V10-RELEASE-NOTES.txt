TrendPilot V10 Commerce and Coupons

Package contents:
- css/style-v10.css
- js/site-v10.js
- automation/install_v10.py

The installer:
- loads current Admitad coupon data on every page;
- matches the best valid saving to the correct merchant and product;
- adds a complete /deals/ page;
- improves search relevance and same-type comparison choices;
- keeps the comparison drawer fully inside mobile screens;
- adds coupons to product cards and side-by-side comparison cards;
- preserves sponsored/nofollow link attributes;
- adds Deals to navigation and sitemap.
