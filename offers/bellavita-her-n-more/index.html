Questions that stop people from clicking

Clear answers before the seller page.

Why buy minis instead of one full-size perfume?

If you already know your signature scent, a full bottle can make more sense. This set is strongest for discovery, variety, travel and gifting: you get eight different 20ml options before deciding which fragrance deserves a larger-bottle commitment.

Can I layer the fragrances?

You can experiment with layering, but smell each fragrance on its own first. Layer lightly and test combinations on a small area so you learn what works for you instead of mixing everything at once.

Is this exact set eight bottles?

Yes. The current Her N’More listing describes 8 × 20ml miniatures: SENORITA, ROSE, DATE, GLAM, HONEY OUD, WHITE OUD, SKAI and FRESH.
