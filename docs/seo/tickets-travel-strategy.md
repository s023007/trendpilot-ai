# TrendPilot Tickets + Travel — Search-First Strategy

## Goal
Expand TrendPilot beyond physical products into event tickets and travel planning pages that can capture high-intent organic search demand and route visitors to verified affiliate partners.

## First campaign
**Manchester United vs Manchester City — 13 September 2026, Old Trafford.**

Why this event:
- Major Premier League derby with strong international fan interest.
- Enough lead time for search discovery before match day.
- Sports Events 365 currently lists the fixture.
- Trip.com has Manchester flight and hotel inventory, making one article capable of supporting ticket + hotel + flight intent.

## Audience priority
1. UK: ticket + stadium + nearby hotel intent.
2. UAE and Saudi Arabia: ticket + flight + hotel weekend intent.
3. Qatar, Kuwait, Oman: same GCC travel bundle intent.
4. Europe: ticket + short-haul travel + hotel intent.

## Content model
Every event page should answer the event query first, then help the visitor complete the trip:

1. Event facts: date, kickoff, stadium, city.
2. Ticket options: tracked reseller or partner links only.
3. Hotel guidance by area, not stale hard-coded prices.
4. Flight planning for priority origin markets.
5. Stadium transport and matchday timing.
6. Buyer checks: ticket source, delivery method, seat details, refund/cancellation rules.
7. FAQ derived from real search intent.
8. Optional short video that answers one practical question and is embedded on the page.

## Affiliate safety
- Never publish an untracked purchase CTA as the primary CTA.
- Never claim an affiliate reseller is an official club seller unless independently verified.
- Resale prices can differ from face value; disclose this clearly.
- Flight and hotel prices change quickly; avoid static price claims unless generated from a current feed with timestamp.

## Reusable opportunity score
Score future campaigns before publication:

- Search intent: 0–5
- Event/product popularity: 0–5
- Affiliate readiness: 0–5
- Geo fit: 0–5
- Visual/video potential: 0–5
- Time-to-event / freshness: 0–5

Prioritize campaigns scoring 22/30 or higher, with **affiliate readiness >= 3**.
