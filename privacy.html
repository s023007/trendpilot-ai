Privacy policy

This policy explains the limited information that can be processed when you use TrendPilot AI.

Information processed

The site can process standard technical information such as page path, device type, approximate country from an analytics provider, referrer and interaction events. Contact messages contain the information you choose to send.

Affiliate click events

When analytics is configured, an outbound affiliate click can record page, placement, advertiser, product name, revenue model and destination host. TrendPilot does not create clicks automatically.

Cookies

The current static site does not require an account. Analytics cookies are used only when a measurement service is configured and should follow the service's consent requirements for the visitor's region.

External sites

Merchants and services have their own privacy policies. TrendPilot is not responsible for their data practices.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact page.
