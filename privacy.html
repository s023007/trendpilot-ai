Privacy Policy

How TrendPilot AI handles contact submissions, technical data and affiliate links.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Scope

This policy explains how TrendPilot AI handles information when you browse the site, use its contact form or follow a link to a third-party retailer, software provider or affiliate partner.

Information you choose to provide

The contact form may collect your name, email address, subject and message. Netlify processes the submission so the site operator can read and respond to it. Do not submit payment data, identity documents or other sensitive information through the form.

Technical and usage information

Hosting providers may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, requested URL and time of access for delivery, security and diagnostic purposes. If analytics or consent tools are added later, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before activation.

Affiliate and third-party links

Some outbound links contain affiliate tracking parameters. When you follow one, the destination service and its affiliate network may use cookies or similar identifiers to attribute a qualifying click or purchase. TrendPilot does not control the destination site's privacy practices, checkout, account data or cookies.

How information is used

  • To deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • To answer messages and correction requests.
  • To diagnose broken pages or links.
  • To measure affiliate performance where permitted by the applicable programme.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Retention

Contact messages and technical records should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for response, security, record-keeping and legal purposes. Retention periods may differ between Netlify, affiliate networks and destination sites.

Your choices

You may avoid the contact form and contact the destination retailer directly. Browser controls can limit or delete cookies, although doing so may affect attribution or site features. Questions or requests about information submitted through TrendPilot can be sent through the contact page.

Children

The site is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child submitted personal information through the site.

Changes

This policy may change when the site adds analytics, newsletters, accounts or other services. The updated date above will be revised when material changes are published.
