Privacy

Privacy information for TrendPilot visitors.

This page explains the main data flows used by the current site. Third-party sellers and services have their own privacy practices when you leave TrendPilot.

Site requests and technical logs

Hosting and security infrastructure can process standard request information such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic data needed to deliver and protect the site.

Information you submit

If you use a contact or email form, the submitted fields are processed so the message or subscription request can be handled. Do not send sensitive financial, account or identity information through a general contact form.

Saved products

The Saved Products feature is designed to store its list in the browser on the device being used. Clearing browser storage or using another device can remove or hide that saved list. It should not be treated as a cloud account or guaranteed price-monitoring service.

Affiliate and third-party links

Outbound affiliate links can include tracking parameters so a network or provider can attribute an eligible action. Once you open a seller, marketplace, ticket provider or software provider, that third party's own privacy and cookie practices apply.

Data sale

TrendPilot does not sell personal information to advertisers. Affiliate commissions are based on eligible tracked actions, not the sale of a visitor's personal profile by TrendPilot.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.

This policy may be updated as site features or service providers change.
